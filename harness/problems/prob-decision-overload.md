## Problem (David's words)
"I have a lot on my mind. I need AI to help me build. I don't have the killer way for me to:
(1) create plans, (2) review output, (3) conduct research, (4) co-ordinate actions."

## Pain
- Feeling overloaded across the day job (Perchpeek) and personal / 02N builds, with no way to
  set it down.
- Reviewing LLM output means copying a passage out, pasting it, and typing the correction.
  There's no way to talk corrections against specific parts.
- Comments on a Claude doc fire one at a time. They should be applied together, with the whole
  document in view.
- Google Docs plus MCP creates a new file on every revision. Obsidian's vaults are friction.
- The Perchpeek actions review page works, but it's tied to `perchpeek-agent` and can't be used
  for other work.
- Building has stalled since starting the new role.

## What good looks like (David's words, condensed)
Work like a CEO: make decisions rather than do the cognitive work of delivery. Delegate problems
as they are identified, and know they will be solved correctly.

## Evidence
First-hand, from the user. One person, so it's validated for dogfooding and unvalidated for
anyone else.
